What we sell

Henry Automate sells subscriptions to football prediction content. Subscribers pay a weekly or monthly fee to join our private VIP Telegram channel, where we post statistical match predictions before each matchday.

We cover 9 leagues, including the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and the Champions League. Each week subscribers get:

  • Match result (1X2), Over/Under 2.5 goals and Both Teams To Score (BTTS) predictions
  • An Acca of the Week with its model probability and fair odds
  • A short explanation of the data behind each pick

Our predictions come from our own statistical (Poisson) model based on recent team form. We also publish a public weekly results record showing every win and every loss.

What we do NOT do: we do not accept bets, hold customer funds, run games, lotteries or prizes, or act as a bookmaker. Customers pay only for access to our prediction content.
