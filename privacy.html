Who we are

Henry Automate runs an automated football predictions service delivered through Telegram. Contact: henryautomate.predictions@gmail.com

Google account data

Our automation connects to Google Sheets only to store and update our own prediction records. It accesses only spreadsheets that the service owner created or opened with the app. We do not access, collect or share any other Google user data.

Subscriber data

If you join our Telegram channel, Telegram shares only basic public details (such as your Telegram username) with us. If you pay for a VIP subscription, your payment is processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card details.

How we use data

We use data only to run the predictions service and manage subscriptions. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

Deleting your data

To ask us to delete any data we hold about you, email henryautomate.predictions@gmail.com.
